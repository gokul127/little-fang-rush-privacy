Little Fang Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Little Fang Rush ("the game") is published by Agantos ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles.

What the game itself stores

The game saves your progress (best score, collected blood, upgrades, completed missions and the sound setting) on your device only. This information is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Ads, analytics and other parties

The game shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics software. It does not use your device's advertising ID, does not send any information over the internet, and shares nothing with anyone.

If a later version adds ads, this policy will be updated first to say what the advertising providers collect.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
